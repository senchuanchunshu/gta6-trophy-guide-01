Stage 1 · 60–80 h

Finish the story

Play Chapters 1–4 and the finale with Jason and Lucia. Story trophies pop as you go — 18 of the 55 are tied to chapters, missions and stranger strands.

Do it now: in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason’s house in the morning and take her balcony meditation invitation. It is the only interaction currently flagged as possibly missable.

Save often: there is no mission replay and 15 manual save slots — keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.

Stage 2 · ~100 h

Reach 100% completion

A Long Road (gold) is the hardest trophy. Check Progress > Stats > 100% Completion in game — you only need the listed portions, not everything.

  • 100 optional tasks · 39 gang operations · 22 bank jobs · 8 bank vaults
  • 15 of 18 card sets · 100 WAiNK scans · 33 underwater discoveries
  • 42 of 42 shooting range golds · 300 of 397 stunts · 70 of 80 challenges
  • 15 of 15 of Bret’s target decks · $250,000 marked bills fenced

Stage 3 · 10–20 h

Clean up the rest

Finish the stranger finals (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), the last meditation spots, and any combat, driving, robbery or money trophies still open.

Then check the list below — whatever is left is your platinum.

Cheats: turning a cheat on disables trophies (and saving) while it is active, so keep the whole run cheat-free.

Missable warning. Only one trophy is currently flagged as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots, including the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia. No confirmed missable list exists yet; the flag comes from KeenGamer’s guide and Planet GTA 6’s reporting. Use the Missable filter below to see it.
